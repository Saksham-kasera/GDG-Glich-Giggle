- **Concept:** Sliding friction was deprecated. The volume puck bounces back and forth between 0% and 100% with elastic momentum and collision sound effects.
- **The Catch:** To lock in a volume, you must click the **"CATCH & LOCK VOLUME"** button.
- **The Irritation:** The button possesses **magnetic cursor repulsion**—it darts away from your mouse whenever you approach within 75px! Even if you catch it, thermal slippage unlocks it after 3 seconds.

### 2. ❄️ Cryogenic Thermodynamic Scrubber (Keep It Warm)
- **Concept:** Newton's Law of Cooling applied to decibels. Volume rapidly plummets toward 0% at all times.
- **The Catch:** The user must **furiously scrub/rub** their cursor or finger back and forth across the track to generate kinetic heat.
- **The Trap:** If you pause for more than 1.8 seconds, the track hits Absolute Zero and **FREEZES IN SOLID ICE**. An emergency ice pick appears, requiring you to click it 8 times (with realistic ice-shattering acoustics) to thaw the volume.

### 3. 🎯 Ballistic Decibel Trebuchet (Slingshot)

If variance exceeds 12%, the permit is stamped **REJECTED**. If you pass, the system warns of a 4-day wait time unless you pay a volume bribe!

### 5. 🎡 Misfortune Rotary Dial (Teflon Slick Wheel)

## 🚀 How to Run

1. Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
2. Or launch a local server:
   ```bash
   python -m http.server 8080
   ```
   and navigate to `http://localhost:8080`.
3. Put on headphones (or keep speakers on), click **"▶️ Play Cupertino Soundstage"**, and experience the world's most inconvenient volume control!
